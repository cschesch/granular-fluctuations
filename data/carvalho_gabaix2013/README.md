# Carvalho and Gabaix (2013)

`source.zip` preserves the complete original AEA archive recovered from its
September 2015 Internet Archive capture. Its URL and SHA-256 are in
`source.json`. The upstream files are extracted into the ignored cache when needed.

The active application in `code/applications/carvalho_gabaix/` uses the public
BEA--BLS proxy from `data/bea_klems/`. The original deposit is retained for
provenance and replication of the authors' calculations. Earlier source
reconstructions and generated exports are in the dated project archive.
