# BEA–BLS production accounts

The two original historical workbooks preserve the 1947–2016 BEA–BLS production
account and its underlying growth-accounting data. The loader reconstructs the
1963–2016 balanced industry panel and checks its averages against Table 11.1.

Source is https://www.bea.gov/sites/default/files/2020-11/KLEMS-Research-Tables-Underlying-Data_0.xlsx.
